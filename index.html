Key achievements

  • Established structured malaria program systems that enabled a smooth transition to RAI4E.
  • Ensured timely diagnosis and treatment under the Thailand National Malaria Treatment Guidelines.
  • Prevented community outbreaks by proactively detecting asymptomatic cases.
  • Achieved comprehensive coverage with long-lasting insecticidal nets (LLIN) across both shelters.

Program leadership and implementation, 2021–2023

  • Led full implementation of Global Fund malaria activities in line with national malaria guidelines.
  • Planned and managed activity-based budgets, including community-level HR.
  • Monitored targets, timelines and performance indicators, coordinating directly with the sub-recipient (SR).

Core activities

  • Established and operated malaria posts for diagnosis and treatment (RDT and microscopy).
  • Ran proactive and reactive case detection in high-risk and index-case areas.
  • Set up a follow-up system so patients completed 4 blood tests within 90 days.
  • Conducted LLIN surveys and distributions and implemented focal indoor residual spraying (IRS).
  • Delivered malaria training and treatment guideline sessions for medics, CHWs, lab staff, MPWs and MOWs.
  • Managed malaria commodity forecasting and stock: RDTs, LLINs, antimalarials, chemicals and supplies.

Reporting and data

  • Entered and managed malaria patient data in the national malaria database.
  • Produced quarterly reports to the SR: narrative reports by targets and budget lines, Malaria Information System datasets (PR format), financial reports (expenditure, forecast, carry-over), and stock and commodity reports.
  • Submitted monthly internal performance reports.
  • Developed and maintained an internal malaria dashboard with monthly updates.
  • Led monthly data validation, combining digital verification and tally checks.
  • Monitored cases and ran slide QC with the Vector Borne Disease Unit (VBDU) to keep treatment in line with national guidelines.
  • Passed regular SR audits and the external Global Fund audit review.

Representation and coordination
